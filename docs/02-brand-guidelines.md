# Brand Guidelines

## Brand Name

Lucía Ramírez

---

## Brand Personality

Professional.

Minimalist.

Modern.

Reliable.

Friendly.

Technical without being intimidating.

---

## Mission

Help small businesses save time by automating repetitive tasks and building software solutions.

---

## Vision

Become a trusted software engineer recognized for creating elegant and useful solutions.

---

## Tone of Voice

Clear.

Simple.

Honest.

Professional.

Approachable.

---

## Design Principles

- White backgrounds.
- Lots of whitespace.
- Elegant typography.
- Smooth animations.
- No unnecessary effects.
- Content first.

---

## Color Palette

Primary: White

Secondary: Near Black

Accent: Blue

Neutral: Light Gray
